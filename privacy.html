Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Knoverse ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Knowear mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account or sign in with Google or Apple, we collect:

  • Name and email address
  • Profile photo (if you add one, or if available from your Google account)
  • Account preferences and settings

1.2 Style Profile

To personalise recommendations you may choose to provide gender, body type and shape, height, weight, body measurements, and skin tone. These are optional.

1.3 Photos

You may upload photos from your camera or photo library. How each photo is handled depends on the feature:

  • Stored in your private account storage: wardrobe item photos, profile photos, Stash (wishlist) items, moodboard images, and Shopping Bestie photos only when you choose to save a verdict to your Diary.
  • Analysed and not stored: photos you check with Shopping Bestie (fitting-room buy/skip advice) or Get the Look (recreating an outfit from your wardrobe) are sent for AI analysis and discarded afterwards unless you save them.

1.4 Wardrobe, Outfit and Wear Data

  • Clothing details such as category, colour, brand, size, material, condition, and purchase price you choose to enter
  • Outfits, outfit plans, saved inspirations, and Get the Look results
  • Wear experiences you record: how you felt (emotions, mood and confidence ratings), fit and comfort feedback, and compliments received
  • Usage such as times worn and last worn

1.5 Calendar Data

If you connect Google Calendar, we read your events (read-only) to display them in the App and suggest outfits for them. We never create, modify, or delete events in your Google Calendar. See section 5 for our Google API Services disclosure.

1.6 Location

With your permission, the App uses your device location to show local weather for outfit suggestions. Your coordinates are sent through our server to weather and place-name services (see section 4) to fetch the forecast and city name. We do not store your location.

1.7 Purchases

If you subscribe to Knowear Plus or Premium, we receive your subscription status (plan, renewal and expiry dates) from the App Store or Google Play via our subscription provider. Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card or payment details.

1.8 Usage and Device Information

  • How often you use AI features (to apply plan limits)
  • Device type, operating system, and App version
  • Push notification tokens (if you enable notifications)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide your digital wardrobe, outfits, calendar planning, and diary
  • Generate AI outfit recommendations, Shopping Bestie verdicts, and Get the Look matches
  • Personalise suggestions to your style profile, weather, and events
  • Manage your account, subscription, and AI usage limits
  • Send notifications you have opted into (e.g. outfit reminders)
  • Send service emails (e.g. welcome email) and occasional product updates about Knowear
  • Respond to support requests and keep the App secure

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for third-party advertising or to track you across other apps and websites.

3. AI Features

Knowear's AI features are powered by a third-party AI provider. Before any of your data is sent to our AI provider, the App asks for your permission. You can use Knowear without AI features, and you can withdraw permission at any time in Settings → Privacy → AI analysis.

Depending on the feature, the following is sent to our AI provider:

  • Shopping Bestie: the photo you are checking, your style profile (section 1.2), and a summary of similar items you own
  • Get the Look: the outfit photo you upload
  • AI Stylist and outfit suggestions: wardrobe item details, occasion, weather, and your preferences
  • Event and trip planning: event details (title, type, time, location, description) and your existing outfits
  • Adding a wardrobe item: the item photo, to auto-fill its details

Requests are made from our secure servers using our AI provider's paid API. Under the provider's terms for this service, it does not use this data to train or improve its models. We do not use your data to train AI models either.

4. Service Providers We Share Data With

We share data only with the providers below, who process it on our behalf to run the App:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage, and server functions
  • Google — Google Sign-In and Google Calendar (if connected)
  • AI provider — a third-party AI service that powers AI features, used only after you allow AI analysis (section 3)
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and App Store purchases
  • RevenueCat — subscription management (receives your account ID and purchase history)
  • Resend — sending emails (receives your name and email address)
  • Open-Meteo and OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) — weather and city name for your location (receive coordinates only, not your identity)
  • Expo — delivering push notifications

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or the public.

5. Google API Services Disclosure

Knowear's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google Calendar data is used only to show your events and plan outfits for them inside the App; it is not sold, not used for advertising, and not read by humans except with your consent, for security purposes, or as required by law. Event details are shared with our third-party AI provider only to generate outfit suggestions you request, and only after you allow AI analysis.

6. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS)
  • Row-level security ensures only you can access your own data
  • Photos are kept in private storage, accessible only to your account
  • API keys for AI and other services are kept on our servers, never in the App

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Delete your account in the App (Settings → Delete Account) or at knoverse.io/delete-account
  • Withdraw AI permission in Settings → Privacy → AI analysis
  • Delete individual items, outfits, diary entries, and photos at any time
  • Disconnect Google Calendar in the App or in your Google Account settings
  • Turn off location or notifications in your device settings
  • Opt out of product update emails by emailing support@knoverse.io
  • Access or correct your data by contacting us

8. Data Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data, including photos, within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it. Photos analysed by Shopping Bestie or Get the Look without being saved are not retained.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we will delete it promptly.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including by the service providers listed above. We take steps to ensure it remains protected in line with this Policy.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes in the App or by email. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
