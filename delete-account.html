Delete Your Account or Data

Knowear App by Knoverse · Last updated 4 October 2026

You can delete your whole Knowear account, or just some of your data while keeping your account. This page explains both options.

Option 1: Delete your account in the app (instant)

  1. Open the Knowear app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to Danger Zone and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and all associated data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request account deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email support@knoverse.io from the email address on your Knowear account with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We'll verify the request and delete your account within 30 days.

Request Account Deletion

Have a Plus or Premium subscription? Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Billing is handled by the App Store or Google Play, so cancel it there first: on iPhone, Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

What Data Will Be Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Account information - your name, email address, and profile
  • Body and style profile - gender, body profile, skin tone, and style preferences
  • Wardrobe items - all clothing photos, descriptions, and metadata
  • Outfits - all outfit combinations you have created, including favourites
  • Calendar and plans - outfit plans, trip plans, internal events, and calendar connections
  • Wear experiences - moods, emotions, fit feedback, and compliments you recorded
  • Shopping Bestie and Stash - saved verdicts, diary photos, and saved items
  • Inspiration - saved inspiration and recreated looks
  • Subscription status - your Plus or Premium status stored by Knowear
  • Preferences and activity - app settings, personalisation, notification tokens, badges, and usage history
  • Photos - all photos you uploaded to Knowear

Delete Some of Your Data (Keep Your Account)

You don't have to delete your account to remove specific data.

In the app, you can delete individual items at any time:

  • Wardrobe items - open an item and delete it
  • Outfits - open an outfit and delete it
  • Outfit plans and events - remove them from your calendar
  • Shopping Bestie diary entries and Stash items - delete them from their lists
  • Calendar connection - disconnect Google Calendar from the Calendar settings screen

By email, you can ask us to delete any specific type of data (for example, all wear experiences, your body profile, or all photos). Email support@knoverse.io from your account email with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and tell us what you'd like removed. We'll complete it within 30 days.

Data Retention

In-app account deletion takes effect immediately. Email requests are processed within 30 days. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Purchase and transaction records held by Apple, Google, and our subscription processor are kept under their own policies and for legal, tax, and accounting purposes. We may also keep limited data where the law requires it, and delete it once that period ends.

Questions?

If you have any questions about deleting your account or data, contact us at support@knoverse.io.
